The linear-ramp initialization helps the ratio at p = 2, 3 (0.703, 0.720) but lowers P(optimal) (0.067, 0.118), and it does nothing for the penalty variant.

The advantage is in the distribution (P(optimal), ratio), not in the best sample. Instances are small, simulator only, mean over only 7 instances, so no claim beyond this benchmark.
  - Dicke runs optimize on the statevector of the Dicke state directly (StatePreparation is ~10x slower per evaluation); the full circuit with StatePreparation is used for sampling and is checked against the Estimator in tests.
