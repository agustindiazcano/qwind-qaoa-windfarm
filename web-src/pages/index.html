Runs on real quantum hardware: IonQ Forte 1 (36q)

A quantum algorithm (QAOA, in Qiskit) that picks which wind turbines to keep running, and never proposes an invalid plan. Tested on the real turbines and measured winds of two operating wind farms.

  • 3 to 7.5 times more likely to find the best layout than a random valid choice, and 3.4 to 17.1 times more likely than the usual penalty formulation.
  • Exact optimum in every instance of 5 of 6 datasets, matching simulated annealing, with about 120 optimizer evaluations.
  • 100% valid plans: the "keep exactly K turbines" rule is built into the circuit.
Mathematical model
QUBO
Turbine selection written as a QUBO that captures wake interactions between turbines.
Algorithm
QAOA
QAOA built by hand in Qiskit, simulated, with the "keep exactly K turbines" rule inside the circuit.
Valid plans
100%
Every output of the XY mixer + Dicke circuit keeps exactly K turbines.
Wind direction

A 16-sector wind rose decides which turbines sit in the wake of which.
Wake effect
Slower wind
A turbine standing in another one's wake gets slower wind and produces less.

1. What is it?

QWind decides which turbines to keep running when a farm must produce less, so wakes waste as little energy as possible.

3. How does the quantum part work?

Always exactly 3 on

  1. 1Start from a valid plan
  2. 2Only swap switches in pairs
  3. 3Tune the circuit (about 120 tries)
  4. 4Sample 1,000 times, keep the best

The usual approach discourages invalid answers with a penalty but still produces many. Ours makes them impossible. Technical names: QAOA with an XY mixer and a Dicke start state.
